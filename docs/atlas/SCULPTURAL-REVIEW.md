## Presentation material and limits

Run `node scripts/capture-atlas-presentation.cjs` against the completed static server, with `PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE` when needed. It writes 66 actual interface captures, including 1600×1200 desktop images, and a real browser recording to ignored `audit/atlas-presentation/`. Credits and source/render/model distinctions are in [SCULPTURAL-ART-DIRECTION.md](SCULPTURAL-ART-DIRECTION.md) and [folio-provenance.json](folio-provenance.json). Automated Chromium, Firefox and Linux WebKit checks support the review. Linux WebKit is an engine check, not certification on a physical iPhone or Safari device. Real-device touch, Safari/Firefox manual review, a final editorial critique and the owner's submission decision remain appropriate before entering an award. No Awwwards entry or submission payment is made, and no award outcome is promised.
